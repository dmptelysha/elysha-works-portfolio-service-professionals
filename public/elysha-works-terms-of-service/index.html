02. Discovery-call booking

When online booking is enabled, you complete the project questions first, choose an available time and then confirm. Provide an email address you control and accurate information you are authorised to share.

Submitting answers or selecting a time does not confirm an appointment. The booking page must show a confirmed appointment after the calendar checks complete. A Rhea reply, a preview, or an old confirmation-page link is not proof of a reservation.

Discovery calls are free and last 30 minutes through Google Meet, with no obligation to purchase a service. Elysha reserves 30 minutes of private rest after each call. The scheduling window is Monday to Saturday, 6:00 PM to 3:00 AM the following day, in Asia/Manila. The time-zone selector displays available times in your selected zone; check it before confirming. Live availability and existing calendar commitments determine what can be booked.

Google handles calendar invitations. Email delivery, spam filtering and a recipient’s calendar settings are outside the booking page’s control. If the confirmed details or meeting link are unclear, contact Elysha Works instead of submitting duplicate bookings.

Cancellation, rescheduling & attendance

You may cancel a call at any time. To qualify for one reschedule per enquiry, you must cancel or request the change at least two hours before the scheduled call, and the allowance must be unused. A cancellation with less notice is still allowed, but does not qualify for a replacement call. Changes are subject to live availability and server eligibility checks.

If you cancelled the original call with enough notice, you may request your one replacement by completing the booking questions again with the same email address. A replacement uses the same one-time allowance; submitting another form does not reset it or guarantee a booking.

If you do not attend the rescheduled discovery call, Elysha will review attendance after the call time. Once the missed call is confirmed, Elysha Works will close that non-client enquiry and will not proceed with that proposed engagement. This is not an automatic judgement by Rhea or a change to an existing client agreement.

Use the private management link supplied with a confirmed booking when available. Keep that link secure. If it has expired or does not work, or you need to notify us of a late cancellation, email support@elyshaworks.com. A cancellation does not automatically delete your enquiry or its answers.

If Elysha needs to change a call, the change should be communicated to you. No monetary cancellation or no-show fee is imposed by these discovery-call terms.
