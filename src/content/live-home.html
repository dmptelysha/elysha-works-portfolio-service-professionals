Do you only work with service professionals?+

Service professionals are my main focus because their websites often need to do more than simply provide information.

They need to build trust, explain the value of an offer, generate inquiries, and help potential clients take the next step.

I also take selected e-commerce and custom web projects when they’re a good fit.

What if I already have a website?+

You may not need to start over.

I can review your current website and identify where the messaging, structure, user journey, or calls to action could be clearer.

From there, we can decide whether you need a focused improvement or a more complete redesign.

Do I need a website or a funnel?+

It depends on how your clients find you and how they decide to work with you.

A website can support your overall business and credibility, while a funnel is usually built around a more specific audience, offer, and action.

We’ll look at your customer journey first before deciding what to build.

Can you build on WordPress?+

Yes.

I build WordPress websites and can add e-commerce functionality through WooCommerce when needed.

Do you work with Shopify?+

Yes.

For e-commerce projects, I can work with Shopify or WordPress + WooCommerce.

The right platform depends on your products, workflow, budget, and what you need the store to do.

Can you connect booking and automation?+

Yes.

Depending on your workflow, your website can connect with forms, appointment booking, notifications, email follow-ups, CRM tools, and other automations.

The goal isn’t to automate everything. It’s to automate the right things.

Absolutely.
